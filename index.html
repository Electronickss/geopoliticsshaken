The Load-Bearing Structure

2026-08-15

The era conservatives romanticize had 52% corporate tax rates and no buybacks. The Nordic model wasn't built on oil or homogeneity; it was built on a deal: share the gains and losses of structural change, and the economy innovates. What broke that deal in the US? SEC Rule 10b-18, the rise of stock buybacks, and a shift from retain-and-reinvest to downsize-and-distribute. Now the velocity of money has collapsed, the top 0.1% captured almost all gains, and antitrust's price-only lens missed the platform monopolies. Economic elasticity , the capacity to absorb shocks and endure , is built by protecting the people who produce the value.
